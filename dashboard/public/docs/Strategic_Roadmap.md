# Strategic Roadmap: 30 / 60 / 90 days (proposal)

*Independent Student Research. Proposed by the student research team; not approved by ThermGAS. Owners are suggested roles, not named people. Gate thresholds are assumptions for management to confirm or change. Source of truth: `data/executive.json`.*

## Days 0-30: Fix the evidence
| Task | Suggested owner | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| Resolve 0.5 vs 1 ppm; DMC calibration and interferent tests; publish spec; add DMC to Master Gas List (R1) | Product / Applications Engineering lead | Test-conditioned DMC spec sheet |
| Write the hazardous-area position (R3) | Engineering lead + safety reviewer | One-page installation position |
| Request quotes: FALCO lamp and service, Riken monitors, ThermGAS configuration (R4) | Business Development lead | Quote file with sources |
| Re-verify HL-GA, Queen Creek, SK Commerce, Toyota NC, Panasonic KS and UBE Louisiana (R6) | Research analyst (student team) | Updated `data/*.json` |
| Schedule 5-8 EHS / process-engineer interviews (R2) | Business Development lead | Interview guide and schedule |

**Success:** spec issued with stated conditions; hazardous-area position written; at least one competitor quote; interviews scheduled.
**Go / No-Go #1 (Day 30).** Go if the spec and position are complete and interferent results do not contradict the selectivity claim. No-Go if results contradict the claim or engineering cannot support the work: stop and reposition before any customer pilot.

## Days 31-60: Test the message and line up a site
| Task | Suggested owner | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| Complete interviews; test the "concentration layer complements event detection" message (R2) | Business Development lead | Interview summary: who owns monitoring, what is budgeted |
| Approach Georgia / Southeast plants with verified or planned production (R5) | Business Development lead + executive sponsor | Two candidate sites with named contacts |
| Draft the pilot protocol (R5) | Field / Applications engineer | Protocol for customer sign-off |
| Verify competitor channels in TN, KY, MI, KS, NC (R6) | Research analyst | Updated state evidence |

**Success:** interview findings recorded; contact at two sites; protocol drafted.
**Go / No-Go #2 (Day 60).** Go if a site agrees in principle and interviews do not reject the message. No-Go: pivot the segment (for example the Louisiana DMC-producer segment) or the positioning.

## Days 61-90: Start the pilot
| Task | Suggested owner | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| Install analyzer; record baseline next to the customer's detector (R5) | Field / Applications engineer | Installation record, baseline readings |
| Weekly log of readings, nuisance alarms, maintenance (R5) | Field / Applications engineer | Pilot log |
| Build the lifecycle-cost comparison from real quotes (R4) | Business Development lead + finance | Documented comparison |
| Early readout for management (R5) | Executive sponsor | Readout and continue decision |

**Success:** analyzer running at one site; baseline and first logs recorded; comparison built only from documented inputs.
**Go / No-Go #3 (Day 90).** Go if the pilot runs without a safety or access objection and the log is usable. No-Go if the site withdraws, a safety objection is raised, or data is unusable.

*A 60-90 day pilot started in this plan finishes after Day 90; the full readout and any commercial decision are outside this window.*

## Business case limits
No revenue, ROI, market share or ThermGAS internal numbers are estimated. Scenarios (evidence sprint, single-site pilot, two-site pilot) define scope and resources as labeled assumptions. Cost drivers without a public source are shown as Unknown. The only price used is ION's $3,521 reseller list. The website's comparator counts detectors and lamp changes from published specs (at least 9 lamp changes per FALCO over 36 months); it makes no cost or savings claim.
